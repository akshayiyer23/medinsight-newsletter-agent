The 2026 Milliman Medical Index: The highest healthcare cost increase in over a decade
The 2026 Milliman Medical Index shows healthcare costs for the average American rose 7.9% to $8,460 this year, the steepest annual increase in more than a decade. For a family of four, total costs hit $37,824, with pharmacy costs surging 14.8% driven by GLP-1 drugs and specialty medications, while outpatient facility costs rose 7.5%. Together those two categories account for 69% of the entire year-over-year increase, and for the first time in the MMI's history, AI-enabled hospital billing was flagged as an emerging cost driver. This is the data environment every payer walking into AHIP needs to understand.
