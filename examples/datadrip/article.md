# Title: How to ensure effective & agile healthcare decision-making
# Byline: Sarah Quinn, Director of Marketing | July 10, 2025

The healthcare industry generates a wealth of data, but establishing the right tools and processes for making sense of it remains a challenge for many healthcare organizations.

Without high-quality analytics, your data can't really be trusted, let alone used to support reliable decision-making or to identify and act on the trends and conclusions the data contains.

When you have the processes and framework in place for ensuring the accuracy and timeliness of your data and using it to make well-informed decisions, you have the tools for significantly reducing medical and financial risks and ensuring better outcomes for patients, providers and payers.

## Building an analytics center of excellence

Transforming your analytic processes into a system of converting healthcare data into meaningful, actionable insights is the goal of establishing a healthcare data analytics center of excellence.

An analytics center of excellence is a framework for transforming data into meaningful insights that can be leveraged across an entire organization for better decision-making. It provides the tools and the personnel to tackle questions with deeper analysis that provides better answers, in less time.

It can deliver on key analytics needs of healthcare organizations including timely insights that ensure your business decisions are not built on out-of-date data, effective storytelling that helps you communicate across a broad audience, data literacy training so everyone understands how you use your data, trust in your data with simple processes that reduce manual errors, disciplined data infrastructure and scalable modeling, and simple tools for frontline workers, management, and executives.

## Getting started

How do you create an analytics center of excellence? It starts with ensuring the right mix of talent. Your center of excellence needs data scientists, of course, but you also need team members who have the skills to communicate data-based insights to stakeholders.

Key members of your healthcare analytics team include data engineers who manage the collection and cleansing of raw data, data scientists who draw insights and build predictive models with AI and ML, analysts and analytic consultants who play a business-facing role developing reports and dashboards, industry consultants who integrate advanced analytics with outside perspectives, business users who are the domain experts and decision makers, and leaders who motivate the team and model data-driven decision-making.

## Training and education

You will also need to incorporate ongoing and accessible training and opportunities to improve both technical and healthcare analytics proficiency, along with maintaining the needed certifications and continuing education.

## Tools and data sets

The right tools and data sets are key to ensuring that your data analytics center is providing the answers and insights you need. Third-party providers can supply high-quality benchmarks and analysis to validate your data. You can also license healthcare data analytics software platforms, health plan claims analytics, and healthcare predictive analytics software, among other tools.

## Goals and metrics

The success of your center will also depend on establishing clear goals, defined metrics, and processes for measuring your progress.

## Leadership buy-in

Finally, ensure that leadership is actively supporting the effort, with incentives for managers and frontline professionals to learn new skills and open lines of communication.

Your analytics center will enable your organization to deliver powerfully compelling stories, with layers of insights you can use to identify opportunities for care improvement and cost savings. A wide array of additional benefits will leave your organization more confident, competitive and efficient, with better allocation of resources and more effective collaboration among departments.
