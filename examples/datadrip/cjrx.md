Blog: Why VBC still fails for mature organizations: what actually separates high performers
For many organizations, the question of whether to participate in value-based care is settled. The harder question is how to manage it. Jonah Broulette and Umang Gupta argue that the most common failure mode today isn't poor clinical performance or too much risk: it is reliable financial insight arriving too late in the year to change the outcome. They lay out what high-performing ACOs do differently, why forecasting is really a financial governance discipline, and what the next phase of VBC maturity looks like.

Blog: CJR-X starts January 2028 (The hospitals that win are starting now)
CMS has proposed the largest episode-based payment model in Medicare's history: a mandatory, nationwide program for lower extremity joint replacements. This deep dive explains how CJR-X works, how it differs from TEAM, how the target price is actually calculated, and the three priorities hospital leaders should act on now: benchmarking against their U.S. Census Division, tightening HCC documentation, and reassessing post-acute partnerships. Baseline data is already accruing, so the work that decides who wins is happening well before reconciliation.
